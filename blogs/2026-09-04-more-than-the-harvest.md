Six years ago, if you had asked me if I'd ever become a hunter, my answer would have been a quick "no." When I started working at MTN OPS, I admired the passion my coworkers had for the outdoors, but I didn't fully understand it. I'd hear stories around the office about early mornings in the mountains, unforgettable hunts, and freezers full of wild game that would feed families for months. It was inspiring, but it still felt like a world I wasn't part of.
